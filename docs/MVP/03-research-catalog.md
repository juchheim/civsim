## 2. The three themes

| Theme | Core question | Strategic purpose | What overinvesting looks like | What neglecting it looks like |
|-------|---------------|-------------------|-------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| **CRAFT** | *How well do we make things?* | Output per worker: tools, materials, buildings, energy, machines | A small, rich, highly productive core with few people and little land | Hands without tools: slow building, poor harvests, weak walls |
| **KIN** | *How many are we, and do we hold together?* | Population caps (food, group size), health, cohesion, Insight | A huge, fast-growing, research-strong population that spreads by fission but is under-tooled and under-defended | Small, sickly, fractious groups that split apart and stall research |
| **REACH** | *How far do we go, and how do we meet others?* | Movement, claims, trade, communication, number, defense, war, diplomacy, science | A thin, sprawling, trade-rich and border-contesting civilization that may overreach | Boxed-in, isolated, unable to claim land or deal with rivals |

## 6. Setting fidelity: how "real" is the catalog?

- Every project is a real historical technique, institution or discovery, or an honest abstraction of several. The **anchor** line in each lineage header records the historical reference.
- Some events are **compressed or merged** for gameplay (e.g. *Telescope & Heliocentrism*; *Flying Shuttle & Spinning Jenny*), and some are **reordered within an era**. The era a project sits in is chosen for gameplay legibility, not strict chronology. *Castle Building* (E4, Iron era) stands for early fortified strongholds and motte-and-baileys, not Edwardian castles.
- **Dates for the oldest technologies are contested**, and the catalog treats them generously. Bow and arrow ~70–64 ka, possibly ~80 ka; dog domestication estimates run from ~15 ka to ~40 ka; the Abri du Maras cord is ~41–52 ka. None of this affects play, because the calendar is abstract.
- **No magic.** Belief and art lineages act only through cohesion, morale, claims and Insight.
