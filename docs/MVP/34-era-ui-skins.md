# 34 · Era UI skins

Every age gives the HUD its own skin: a low-contrast raster texture on panel surfaces, a tinted hairline border, corner flourishes and a crest on the big sheets. The skin follows the **viewer's own age** and cross-fades when they enter a new one. The oxblood-brown base, the terracotta accent and all text colours are unchanged, so contrast is unchanged. Skins only add detail on top.

## 1. Files

Three images per age, in `game/assets/ui/skins/<slug>/`. Names are exact; the loader looks for nothing else. If a file is missing, that part of the skin is simply skipped.

| File | Size (px) | Purpose | Format rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| `panel_tex.png` | 256 × 256 | Seamless tile drawn over panel surfaces and the research-web stage. | RGBA. The **pattern is carried by alpha** (mean alpha 8–14 %, peak ≤ 35 %). RGB is a light colour (bone, tint) so it shows on the dark surface. Must tile with no visible seam on both axes. |
| `corner.png` | 96 × 96 | Top-left corner flourish. The game flips it for the other three corners and draws it at 48 × 48. | RGBA, transparent background, the motif hugs the top-left corner and its strokes run along the top and left edges, fading out. Era tint colour, fully opaque strokes (the game applies the opacity). |
| `crest.png` | 384 × 24 | Centred ornament on the top edge of sheets and the age banner. The game draws it at 192 × 12. | RGBA, transparent background, left-right symmetric, hairlines fade to transparent at both ends, centre motif. Era tint colour. |

## 2. Where the code reads them

- `game/assets/ui/skins/<slug>/` is the only location.
- `.import` files are generated by Godot (`Godot --headless --path game --import`) and are committed, as for `assets/ui/hud`.
- `game/Scripts/Ui/UiSkins.cs` holds the age table (slug, tint, strengths) and loads textures lazily.
- `game/Scripts/Ui/UiSkin.cs` draws the overlay on panels and does the cross-fade.

## 3. Prompts for hand-painted replacements (optional)

The shipped images are procedural. If you want painterly versions, generate them with these prompts, give them exactly the file names and sizes above, and drop them over the procedural ones. Re-run the import (open the project in Godot once, or the command in §2). Common style words, appended to every prompt:

> flat 2D game UI ornament, no perspective, no text, no lettering, no logo, no border frame, centred crisp strokes, single colour on a plain transparent background

**Textures** (`panel_tex.png`, 256 × 256). Ask for "seamless tileable texture, top-down, even lighting, low contrast, no focal point, no vignette". Most image models do not give a transparent background, so generate on mid-grey and tell me: I convert luminance to alpha. Prompts:

- Dawn: "seamless tileable texture of rough cave-wall rock with soot and faint ochre hand-smudges"
- Hunters: "seamless tileable texture of tanned animal hide with fine grain and faint stitched seams"
- Roots: "seamless tileable texture of woven reed basketwork, tight plain weave"
- Bronze: "seamless tileable texture of hand-hammered bronze sheet, shallow overlapping dimples"
- Iron: "seamless tileable texture of forged dark iron plate, fine brushed grain, faint scorch marks"
- Faith & Fief: "seamless tileable texture of aged vellum with visible fibres and very faint gothic window tracery"
- Discovery: "seamless tileable texture of foxed antique chart paper with fine engraved hatching"
- Industry: "seamless tileable texture of cast iron with fine pitted grain and sparse small rivets"
- Modern: "seamless tileable texture of smooth concrete with fine aggregate speckle"
- Information: "seamless tileable texture of a faint dot grid with thin circuit-board traces"

**Corners** (`corner.png`, 96 × 96): "an ornamental corner flourish for the top-left corner of a panel, strokes hugging the top and left edges, [motif], era tint colour [hex]" with the motif from the table.

**Crests** (`crest.png`, 384 × 24, wide and thin): "a horizontal ornamental divider, perfectly left-right symmetrical, thin lines fading out to both ends, [motif] in the centre, colour [hex]". Ask for 4× size (1536 × 96) and downscale.

For corners and crests, generate on a flat contrasting background and tell me; I key it to transparent.

## 4. Title-screen Start Game button

Not an age skin; it uses the brand colours (oxblood, terracotta, gold) so it sits well on the sunset painting. `game/Scripts/Ui/UiTitleButton.cs` draws it from three images in `game/assets/ui/title/`, generated by `tools/skin-gen/gen_title_button.py`:

| File | Size (px) | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `start_plate.png` | 720 × 144 | Plate at rest, shown at 360 × 72: textured oxblood, terracotta border, gold hairline with diamonds. |
| `start_plate_hot.png` | 720 × 144 | Same shape, warmer; fades in on hover. |
| `start_wing.png` | 220 × 144 | Left scroll flourish (lyre volutes echoing the logo spiral, two leaves), shown at 110 × 72; the game mirrors it for the right side. |

The plate is drawn at a fixed size, not nine-sliced. To change the button's size, change `PlateWidth`/`PlateHeight` and the generator together. Hover warms the plate and slides the wings out; the wings drift slowly at rest (off with Reduce motion). If any image is missing the button falls back to the plain Secondary style.

Preview without the intro video: `Godot --path game -- --title-shot=/tmp/title.png [--title-state=hover|pressed]`.

Prompts for painted replacements (generate on a plain contrasting background and tell me, I key it out): "ornate horizontal button plate, dark oxblood leather with fine grain, thin terracotta border, inner gold hairline with small diamond ornaments, chamfered corners, flat 2D game UI, no text" (plate) and "left half of a gold scrollwork flourish, lyre-shaped double spiral volutes ending in a flat stem that extends right, two small leaves, flat 2D game UI, no text" (wing).
