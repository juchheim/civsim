# 03b — Research Catalog: KIN

> **Theme purpose: People and cohesion.** Kin decides how many people your land can feed, how healthy and long-lived they are, how big a group can hold together before it splits, and how much Insight your people generate. A Kin-leaning civilization booms in population, fissions across the map and out-researches its neighbors.
> Notation is in [02-research-structure.md §16](02-research-structure.md#16-catalog-notation). Overview is in [03-research-catalog.md](03-research-catalog.md).

Lineages: [FOR](#for--foraging--plant-lore) · [HNT](#hnt--hunting--fishing) · [AGR](#agr--agriculture) · [HUS](#hus--animal-husbandry) · [FOD](#fod--food-craft--storage) · [MED](#med--medicine--health) · [SOC](#soc--kinship--society) · [GOV](#gov--governance--law) · [BEL](#bel--belief--ritual) · [ART](#art--arts--culture) · [LRN](#lrn--learning--education)

### The three population caps (why Kin matters)
Population in a settlement is limited by the **lowest** of:
1. **Food:** sustainable food income and stores (FOR, HNT, AGR, HUS, FOD).
2. **Housing:** shelter capacity (BLD, from Craft).
3. **Group cap (cohesion):** the largest group that can hold together. When a settlement exceeds its group cap, it **fissions**: a daughter band leaves to found a new camp, and that is how territory grows (SOC, GOV, BEL, ART).

Health (MED) works on births and deaths, and learning (LRN) multiplies Insight and adoption speed.
