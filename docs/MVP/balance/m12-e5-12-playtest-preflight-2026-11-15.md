## Uncoached comprehension study

Run only after the integrated Fief view is available and the WP-E5-11 promotion gate passes. Recruit eight people who have not been coached on the E5 mechanics. Give the same final build and natural-start task to each person; do not explain mill allocation, fuel, faith, or money during the session. Record an anonymous participant ID, build/pack hashes, prior strategy-game familiarity, the exact prompt, and the participant's verbatim response. Do not record names.

Ask these two open questions after play:

1. “What limited production or work in one town you observed, and what made it a bottleneck?”
2. “Describe one effect you observed from a faith or money system.”

Score each participant only when, without hints, they can explain both:

- One real mill/fuel constraint, such as a mill needing an eligible site and power, shared power-hours limiting competing jobs, or a blast furnace needing charcoal.
- One real faith or money effect present in the tested build, such as staffed faith/learning institutions affecting knowledge, or a trade claim being collected from non-food goods after the protected food reserve.

The comprehension gate passes only if at least **5 of 8** participants satisfy both criteria. Preserve all eight responses and the scoring rationale, including failures. If the tester did not encounter an effect, score that category as not demonstrated; do not teach the mechanic and then count the answer as uncoached.

No participant outreach, sessions, or results are included in this preflight. The study requires real human participants and cannot be completed by simulation output.
