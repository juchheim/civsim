These are not acceptance results, balance fits, or completion of WP-E5-6. No E5 row is promoted.

**WP6 institution opportunity diagnostic, 2026-11-14:** A separate matched Fief cohort compared no institution, one monastery, and one university across 16 fixed seeds (`20261114`–`20261129`), with the same `KEY-5`, `BEL-10`, `LRN-05`, and `LRN-08` projects and one common setup season. All arms had 559 working-age residents total across the cohort (34.94 per fixture). The baseline assigned 0 monks and 0 scholars; monastery assigned 64 monks total (4.00 per fixture); university assigned 16 scholars total (1.00 per fixture). The institution assignments consume those resident adult slots from ordinary work. Source inputs recorded 0 monk/scholar equivalent heads in baseline, 4 monk heads in monastery, and 1.5 scholar-equivalent heads per university (the staffed scholar's existing +50% Fief university input). Catalog-weighted institution role Insight input was 0, 16, and 72 per year per fixture, respectively, before downstream research-system calibration/modifiers; the sum of source role heads times catalog Insight coefficients was 20, 36, and 92 per year per fixture. These are local, one-season fixture outputs only. The sample does not estimate ordinary work's lost output, construction/upkeep costs over time, or whole-match research pacing, and it does not promote draft role values or institution costs.

## Implemented slice

- A monastery is a Fief-only Iron-work entry unlocked by BEL-10. The current first-pass gate requires a town with at least 50 residents, permits at most three monasteries per civilization, and uses the registry's 30 work, 12 block, and 6 wood costs. The three-per-civilization cap is provisional and bounds the first role-output pass; it has not been balanced.
- Each monastery opens four Monk posts. Monks are resident adults assigned real roles and removed from other work. Existing role staffing reserves at least four additional adults for ordinary work; scholars have higher assignment priority. An unstaffed monastery has no monk output.
- The monk role contributes 4 Insight per head per year in the registry, so four staffed posts supply 16 base Insight per year before existing research archetype and role scales. The role is wired from the economy's staffed head count into research inputs. It is inert outside Fief.
- Completed LRN-07 adds one additional Scholar post to each existing monastery. It reuses monastery staffing and Insight inputs; it does not add a separate school building or establish the value of its learning contribution.
- An empty monastery is included in the existing idle-institution count.

The focused monastery tests cover Fief-only construction gating, population/town/project and civilization-cap conditions, four-head staffing, research-role input, non-Fief inertness, and snapshot continuation. University tests cover the added Fief scholar post, local 1.5x Insight effect, and snapshot continuation. The added hospital tests cover its Fief/MED-07 gate, one-physician/two-attendant staffing, the 20% hazard effect on existing outbreak mortality, no effect while unstaffed, and snapshot continuation. The 16-pair forced-Plague diagnostic now records pre-mortality exposures and paired deaths, but is not whole-match or balance acceptance evidence. The 16-seed institution diagnostic records assigned adult roles and source Insight inputs but not lost ordinary work output or whole-match pacing. The Fief economy snapshot version is now 36 for appended Fief-only structures and goods. Bronze and Iron remain at version 30 and retain their original Iron structure block length.

**MED-08 quarantine slice:** completed MED-08 applies a keyed 1-in-2 route-import gate, a separate 3-in-4 imported-outbreak roll (`outbreak_roll ×0.75`), and halves sister-settlement spread probability from 5% to 2.5% in Fief. Separate deterministic streams keep local ambient outbreak incidence unchanged. Iron and Bronze keep their prior behavior. Focused tests cover both import gates, their combined probability, chapter isolation, and snapshot continuation for a pending accepted import. The effects and rates remain draft; this test does not measure whole-match epidemic impact.

The hospital is a Fief-only, MED-07 town structure with the current first-pass 24 work, 8 block, and 4 wood cost. The staff roles are one Physician and two Attendants, assigned as resident adults off other work. While all three posts are staffed, infected figures in that settlement receive 0.8x the existing epidemic hazard. It does not change outbreak incidence, infection, spread, or duration. The costs and reduction are draft values; MED-08 route/spread behavior is recorded separately above.

**WP-E5-6 wilderness-farming slice, 2026-11-14:** BEL-10 now derives up to one wilderness plot per staffed monk, capped at six per monastery. Candidate tiles are within eight tiles of the monastery settlement, outside every settlement's radius, settled field and settlement site, and globally assigned to only one staffed monastery by distance and settlement-id tie-break. Each plot contributes a provisional 0.25 of its biome's normal-field base yield as annual Grain; this value is an unfit draft hypothesis. Assignments are recomputed at harvest and add no snapshot/hash state. Focused coverage checks plot count against four staffed monks and the six-plot cap, uniqueness, footprint exclusions, expected yield, and no Iron effect. The targeted test passes 1/1 and the sim Release build passes with 0 warnings/errors. This is implementation evidence, not food-balance acceptance; no catalog row is promoted.

**WP-E5-6 BLD-12 Gothic Cathedral slice, 2026-11-14:** The Fief Wonder tail now includes Gothic Cathedral (120 draft work, 40 G-ASHLAR, 20 G-WOOD, 4 G-GLASS), using the existing one-world Wonder path and requiring a town, BLD-11, STN-13, and a staffed Master Mason. STN-13's typed large-stone-work speed applies only while that role is assigned. Four focused regressions pass, covering eligibility/staffing, material conservation, world-cap behavior and cohesion/trade scope, plus Fief snapshot continuation. **WP-E5-6 distinct LRN-07 cathedral-school slice, 2026-11-14:** Fief now has a separate `cathedral_school` structure, gated by LRN-07, Fief mode, and town tier, with one school per town. Its draft construction hypothesis is 30 work, 12 block, and 6 wood (matched to the registry monastery scale; the catalog/registry rows remain unchanged). Schools open real adult Scholar seats; the monastery seat remains, and LRN-08 University seats retain their prior behavior. LRN-07's typed 1.5 Scholar-cap modifier uses a deterministic integer rule: for each settlement, start with one seat per built school/monastery, count staffed institutions (a monastery with four Monk adults; a school after at least one Scholar is assigned there), then add the half-capacity seats rounded to nearest integer with halves up. Thus a single staffed institution gives two Scholar seats, two staffed institutions give three, and unstaffed sites never generate the LRN-07 bonus. Three school-focused regressions and the nine existing university/monastery tests pass within the combined 12-test run. Snapshot version is now 38 because the school structure is a second Fief-only Iron structure-tail addition; Iron/Bronze remain version 30.

**WP-E5-6 BEL-11 pilgrimage trip slice, 2026-11-14:** Fief now supports a deterministic completed trip over connected completed road/bridge spokes between a civilization's settlements, gated by BEL-10, BEL-11, and TRN-07. The provisional trip uses a four-adult party and charges one ration per traveler per eight traveled road tiles (minimum one travel season); insufficient food refuses the order without partial consumption. Completing the trip does not reveal fog. A transient keyed route marker scopes the 1.15 route-trade factor to caravans on that settlement pair, composes the Cathedral's 1.1 only when the pilgrimage ended at that Cathedral, and adds the 5% claim emission only on traveled road tiles. The route marker is included in the Fief-only politics hash/snapshot tail and survives midseason restore; Bronze/Iron keep their prior political serialization. Focused coverage passes 4/4 for road/prerequisite gating, food conservation, no fog, route/destination/claim scopes, snapshot continuation, and mode isolation. Party size/cadence remain draft hypotheses; no rows or goldens changed. Current Fief economy snapshot version is 41; Iron/Bronze remain 30.

**WP-E5-6 BEL-10 local Know-how retention, 2026-11-15:** Fief now attributes each season's generated, unspent Know-how to local settlement buckets using actual practice by settlement. The civilization pool remains authoritative for research; consumption debits local buckets in ascending settlement ID, then the explicit civilization reserve. On capture or raze, the local bucket is removed from its former owner; if the last resolved state had a monastery with at least four assigned Monks, the exact unspent balance moves to that former civilization's reserve, otherwise it is discarded from its spendable pool. The captor receives no Know-how and completed technology remains civilization-owned. Fief economy snapshots append and hash the local buckets/reserves at version 41; version-40 Fief snapshots migrate each old pool to the capital, then lowest-ID owned settlement, or to reserve if no settlement remains. Iron/Bronze remain version 30 and keep their existing snapshot/hash paths. `FiefKnowHowRetentionTests` pass 6/6 for generation/spend conservation, staffed and unstaffed capture/raze, technology ownership, snapshot continuation/migration, and mode isolation. This verifies mechanism and determinism only; capture/collapse balance and whole-match pacing still need measurement.
