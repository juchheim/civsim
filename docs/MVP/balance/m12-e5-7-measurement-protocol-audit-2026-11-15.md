## Comparison with established acceptance practice

Earlier acceptance work separated candidate fitting from untouched acceptance evidence and treated one natural world as four independently classified seats. M11 I-T6 froze source, pack, seed block, cutoffs, horizon, and classifications before inspecting outcomes, then ran 80 whole matches / 320 seats. It retained all raw outputs and reported route-censored and no-route seats separately; those outcomes were not passes. It stopped a failed futility candidate without relabeling its 47 completed matches as acceptance. See [M11 I-T6 whole-match method](m11-it6-whole-match-2026-11-05.md).

The earlier focused-diagnostic standard requires estimands, exposure denominators, windows, terminal classifications, and censoring rules to be stated before interpreting data. It explicitly rejects turning descriptive values into pass thresholds without an approved comparison and tolerance. See [M11 I-T5/I-T7 diagnostics](m11-it5-it7-focused-diagnostics-2026-11-06.md). The F-T1 preflight also keeps its 20-world calibration block separate from a frozen, untouched 80-world / 320-seat holdout, and rejects using the holdout to retune a candidate. See [F-T1 final-code preflight](m12-wp5-fief-ft1-final-code-preflight-2026-11-15.md).

WP-E5-7 currently has draft numeric conventions but no approved acceptance bands. A calibration sample can estimate feasibility and event exposure; it cannot create an acceptance criterion after outcomes are visible. The 80-match structure is a suitable minimum whole-match evidence block by precedent, not a magic sample size that ensures rare GOV/DIP events occur. Report effective denominators and uncertainty; if exposure is sparse, retain the result as inconclusive and pre-register a new block instead of inflating denominators with ineligible seasons.

The reserved WP-E5-12 80-seed block (`33,000,000–33,000,079`) is expressly post-WP-E5-11 promotion and human-study adjacent. It is not a WP7 calibration or holdout, and must not be repurposed. Existing AI, F-T5, F-T6/T7, D20, and earlier E5 evidence also cannot serve as a new WP7 block without matching exact provenance and a documented measurement contract.

## Proposed protocol (pre-registration required)

1. **Freeze mechanics and estimands first.** Keep each decision's draft status visible. Before running, record for every measure: target rule/version, unit of analysis, eligible exposure, numerator/denominator, match horizon, event timing, terminal/censor classifications, and any planned contrasts. Carry the autonomous provisional interpretation below into WP-E5-11; do not leave the gate waiting for a new threshold approval. Retain its explicit not-separately-user-approved label.
2. **Use disjoint blocks.** Reserve an unscreened 20-whole-match calibration block for instrumentation/exposure feasibility and fitting/refitting draft numeric parameters. Confirmed mechanics remain fixed. Freeze the resulting candidate, measurement definitions, and acceptance table before running a separate unscreened 80-whole-match holdout (320 seats) for any formally accepted whole-match measures. Generate and hash both manifests before their respective outcomes are inspected, and prove disjointness from all prior E5/WP5/WP8/WP9/D20 ranges and the reserved WP-E5-12 manifest. Do not reuse an inspected holdout after a source/rule/pack/criteria change.
3. **Run natural matches, preserve all outcomes.** Use one match per manifest seed, standard four seats, natural Fief progression and the agreed fixed horizon, without project grants or geography/pacing screening. Keep one raw log per match plus replay/checksum where supported. Include no-event and zero-exposure rows. Report early ends, right censors, no-key/no-route/no-eligibility observations separately and never count them as passes for an event measure they did not expose.
4. **Report uncertainty and dependence honestly.** Summarize at both match and seat level, but resample/cluster by whole-match seed when estimating uncertainty because four seats share a world. For rare events, show exact counts and exposure denominator with an interval; distinguish zero observed events from a proven zero hazard. Keep calibration descriptions separate from the untouched holdout result.
5. **Freeze acceptance separately.** Apply the autonomous working criteria below after they are included in the pre-registration and before opening the holdout. If a measure still lacks a defined comparison or its exposure/precision is inadequate, call it descriptive/inconclusive and leave WP-E5-7 open. No WP-E5-7 evidence changes catalog status; complete WP-E5-11 all-50-row promotion/acceptance and WP-E5-12 later human review remain separate.

These tolerances are not separately user-approved. They preserve the confirmed mechanics, treat the explicitly draft parameters as candidates to fit, and use the established 20-match calibration / 80-match holdout structure. ## Runner and instrumentation gaps

The existing `--fief-institution-trace` remains specific to F-T6. The isolated implementation described below adds a separate `--fief-wp7-trace` path and provenance-guarded runner.

The trace uses optional observers that are not hashed, snapshotted, or read by decisions. It currently captures GOV-10 keyed check rows; GOV-11 city-season and birth-attempt rows; TRD-08 origination, protected-reserve, payment, write-off, and expiry rows; TRD-09 assigned admin cost/release and delivered margin rows; DIP-08 eligible keyed hit, pending, confirmation/reversal, and succession rows; and SOC-11 per-recipe inputs, output, quality, and training rows. The runner hashes the source tree, pack, Release Harness and adjacent Sim against the manifest, refuses existing output directories and mismatches, writes a raw log per match as the process runs, and updates exit/hash metadata after each seed. An interrupted seed remains marked `running` with its raw file preserved.

The current narrow trace still has limits: GOV-11 emits a same-state derived multiplier and paired fertility-input counterfactual, but not paired whole City-year population outcomes; DIP-08 exposes one row per eligible claimant check (multiple eligible allies can yield multiple rows for one neutral settlement-season), so its acceptance denominator needs explicit treatment; TRD-09 logs per-good seller/buyer stock deltas but does not supply paired trace-on/off snapshots of offers and accepted terms; and SOC-11 reports assignment-level craft/smelting training exposure but does not prove effects on tool durability or T12 life. Those gaps prevent this trace alone from satisfying every acceptance field. The runner's full-match path is implemented but has only been smoke-tested with one one-minute match, not a calibration or holdout block. No candidate values have been fit from a cohort.
