== 8. Limits ==
Static starts: expansion adds settlements, and the sim measures [Seen] from every settlement, so real exposure is at least what is reported here and grows with the realm (one fission hop adds up to 14 tiles).
A seam is evidence, not a deposit: the generator places guaranteed seams on barren host-rock tiles near the start pair and never on coal, so a seen seam does not promise a coal tile nearby. Section 4 reports the deposit geography separately.
Era reach counts the era at which MIN-09 is researched (E6, 35). The Seen mask is cumulative, but E6 has the largest reach before it, so it dominates. WAY-01 (E0) is assumed done by E6, so the 52 row is the realistic one.
Contact and barter can also set the gate (Coal is a traded good; a contact that finished MIN-09 makes it known), and explorers set it when they cross a coal deposit tile; neither is modeled here beyond the mate and world rows of section 3.
All four starts per world are two geometric configurations (civ 2 and 3 mirror civ 0 and 1), so 'per world' figures rest on 20,000 distinct starts, not 40,000 independent ones.
