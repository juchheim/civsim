## A8 false-row diagnosis status and required telemetry

The 20 interim `pass:false` rows are **inconclusive**, not proof of a production visibility leak and not a pass. Eleven are `unseen-composite` rows and nine are `visible-fleet` controls. The active run has not completed. Current `a8-rows.jsonl` records category counts and truncated observer-state diffs, but it omits the identities and visibility timing needed to distinguish a source defect from the fixture moving into view before the checkpoint.

The relevant source boundary is explicit: `BeliefBuilder.Update` observes a rival settlement at season end only when it was visited, is within routine sight, or its scout owner-seen season is current. Its `Observe` method is where rival settlement population, defenses and `SeenFlag.Harbor` are copied into belief. The A8 perturbation picks “unseen” targets using pre-step sight, then advances the entire world through a season-end update; a scout can reveal a target between those points. Thus a post-step Harbor/population/defense diff alone cannot distinguish valid newly visible evidence from a leak. `OwnViewBuilder.Build` refreshes `CrewedGalleyHomes` at season end when `IronChain` is active, and `CompareOwnView` compares the list; the current visible-fleet rows lack the before/after lists and galley target needed to explain an applied control with no diff.

For a diagnostic rerun, add test-only telemetry to each row, without changing the acceptance thresholds or visibility rules:

- For each unseen-composite perturbation: exact changed tile, settlement, civilization, and category IDs; pre-step explored/owner-seen state; routine and figure distances; scout owner-seen season; contact/nearest-settlement skip decision; and the same sight fields at the checkpoint immediately before `AiSystem.OnSeasonEnd` updates the observer.
- For each unseen-composite mismatch: full untruncated diff list; observer belief/decision hash before and after; any changed target's `LastSeenSeason`, `SettlementSeen`/`FiguresSeen`, scout visit, and map-owner record; plus whether the changed field was actually observed this season. Record these for both baseline and perturbed copies.
- For each visible-fleet control: target settlement ID, civ, `IronChain`, harbor/galley structure counts, supplied crew, and `CrewedGalley` immediately before and after fixture application; then the baseline and perturbed `CrewedGalleyHomes` lists and `OwnView.Tick`/season before and after the step. This will reveal whether the fixture was already equivalent to baseline, the own view failed to refresh, or the observable lists actually differed.
- For all row kinds: emit every diff, explicit `notApplied` reason, checkpoint tick, baseline/perturbed pre- and post-step checksums, raw row count and a final summary with applied denominators, visible-control misses, censors/errors, exit code, and output hash.
