## F-T3 (unchanged rules: <=15% in every mature rolling 12-season window, zero furnace-attributed crossings, fuel-short <=5%)

**Cause of the regression** from the 2026-11-14 pass (36/36, max 14.94%, 0.06 pt of margin): the later SOC-11 craft-loss allowance (5% extra input per Fief recipe) also taxed ore reduction and metal refining (bloomery, smelting, alloying, wrought iron, steel), lengthening the kit build-out burst in seasons 3-16. The no-furnace control reaches about 22% in that window, so the furnace was never the cause.

**Fix, in two parts**
1. `FiefCraftLossApplies`: the SOC-11 allowance is a loss of "non-kit crafted goods" (its catalog note), so ore reduction and refining (`smelt_*`, `cosmelt_*`, `alloy_bronze`, `bloomery`, `forge_wrought`, `carburize_steel`) are exempt, as tool kits already were. Alone it gave 35/35 on the 39 fit seeds (max 15.0%) but 37/39 on the fresh 40 (max 15.6%) and 36/38 on a new 40 (max 15.3%).
2. Fief furnace bloomery labor factor at full coverage fitted from the catalog's x2 to **x1** (`FurnaceLaborFactorAtFullCoverage`; the x4 batch needs the same crew labor, so labor per litre falls to a quarter, the same direction as the already-fitted charcoal factor x1). ## F-T2 second windmill

Natural cohort (evidence copy, 4 of 16 seeds) had two sub-60% windows (seeds 20700066 s4 54.3%, 20701208 s5 36.5%), each with one windmill and no second source in 12 seasons although the D12 gate was open (`wind-hard=open; wind-wanted=open`) and sites and fields were fine. Cause: a Fief town builds one Iron structure at a time in enum order, and materials are planned only for the current build, so the wanted second windmill waited behind other works and its wheel and blocks were in store in 1 season of 12.

**Fix** (`FiefMillDeficitKind`, Iron.cs): a Fief town that already has a mill and whose existing coverage is under the draft 60% screen (the existing D12 appetite gate, cap two) (a) puts the windmill first in selection ahead of the defense-first list, (b) preempts a current build (progress shelved, as always) once the windmill's materials are in store, and (c) plans its wheel and blocks while another build is current. No new threshold; the 60% screen, two-source cap and 260 residents per source are unchanged.

Reruns of the two failing seeds on the dev build (130 minutes, `--fief-power-ledger`): seed 20700066 8/8 windows at or above 60% (88.0% weighted; one window with two windmills, 74.8%); seed 20701208 10/10 (87.6%). These are single-seed checks of the previously failing towns; the full 16-seed natural cohort on the final source is recorded in the closeout ledger.

Tests added: `A_town_below_the_grain_screen_puts_its_second_windmill_ahead_of_another_build_and_plans_its_wheel_and_blocks`, `The_windmill_does_not_jump_the_queue_once_coverage_is_met_or_in_Iron` (`FiefPowerTests` 15/15).
