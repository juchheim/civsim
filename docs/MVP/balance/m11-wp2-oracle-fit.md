## Decision and next inputs

WP2 produced reproducible recipe, replacement, route, cap, conservation, combat-stack and state-accounting checks. It did **not** produce an E4 balance fit or any passing I-target. Do not promote numeric YAML or use these fixture values as a live result. WP3–WP12 still need the mode-aware simulator, full supply/access ledgers, live E4 trajectories and held-out evidence specified in 25.

The E3 anchor is deliberately additive: historical `live-e3-baseline.csv`, current E3 content, and golden files remain untouched. The E3 24-seed and two 24-match politics blocks are fresh diagnostics; neither substitutes for the >=80-match evidence required for noisy conclusions. All new oracle modules are additive under `docs/MVP/balance/tools/`.
