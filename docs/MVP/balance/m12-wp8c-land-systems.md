# M12 WP-E5-5 (fourth slice): land, herds and claimed grain (2026-11-13)

**Status:** The remaining first-pass system C mechanics are implemented on `codex/e5-fief`, alongside the earlier rotation, horse-collar, and power/furnace slices. These rules are gated on Fief mode after KEY-5; no new persistent or hashed state was added. E5 catalog rows remain draft.

## Rules

| Project | First-pass effect | Scope and limits |
|---|---|---|
| HUS-10 Selective Breeding | Domestic meat, milk and wool yield ×1.5; ox and collar-horse draft strength ×1.2. | Fief after KEY-5 only. Wild-game harvest is unchanged. |
| HNT-11 Game Reserves & Falconry | Game on a tile owned by a Fief civilization with HNT-11 keeps at least half its local carrying capacity; other civilizations cannot hunt or tame it. The owner needs two assigned adults to hunt there, representing a hunter and falconer. | Reserve ownership is derived from tile ownership and the herd's location; there is no separate reserve building. A nearby claimed reserve adds +2 local cohesion. |
| TER-10 Manorial Estates | 10% of actual harvest from owned field tiles is transferred as grain to the nearest staffed same-civilization town or city. TER-10 changes the rate to 7%. | A work-capable adult is the proxy for non-farming staff; jobs and grain stores are pooled by settlement. No payment is made when no other staffed town or city can receive it. |
| WOD-10 Timber Framing & Trusses | Hall housing capacity ×1.5. | Fief after KEY-5 only. Halls are the existing modeled building-size surface; separate barn, shipyard and workshop size caps do not exist yet. |

AGR-11's deterministic three-field rotation and NRG-04's horse collar remain as described in [m12-wp8a](m12-wp8a-land-rotation.md) and [m12-wp8b](m12-wp8b-horse-collar.md). The existing power/furnace work is described in [m12-wp8-power-furnace](m12-wp8-power-furnace.md).

Output is in [m12-wp8c-fief-fuel-output](m12-wp8c-fief-fuel-output.txt). Eight seeds had mature windows; all eight stayed below 15% metal-chain work. The maximum was 14.3%. NoFuel was 0% in eligible windows, forest-ledger residuals were 0.000 L, and the furnace added no forest-clear crossings. Seed `20261026` produced no kit or charcoal-demand seasons, so it is censored. Two seeds in total did not meet the I-T6 minimum charcoal-demand-season count.
- The expanded matched F-T3 screen originally reported 26/33 mature pairs above 15%, but its fixture reserved power for sawing without a WOD-09 sawmill. That phantom demand left only 1.6 of 4.0 craft hours for the furnace. Sawing demand is now gated on an available mill source and a built, WOD-09 sawmill; focused tests cover absent, unresearched and eligible sawmill cases. The pre-fix output remains archived as invalid evidence. The corrected 39-seed rerun uses the same promoted pack, 200-person town, spatial controls and 64-season window; raw output: [m12-wp8c-fief-fuel-corrected-39-output-2026-11-14.txt](m12-wp8c-fief-fuel-corrected-39-output-2026-11-14.txt). Of 39 pairs, 32 had mature rolling windows and 17 exceeded the unchanged 15% maximum-window target; the maximum was 16.8% (seed `20261054`, seasons 6–17). The first eligible windows exceeded 15% in 16/32 mature seeds (maximum 16.8%); wholly late windows (ending season 44 or later) all passed, with a maximum of 2.5%. Seven seeds had no mature window. Fuel shortfall was 0/30 eligible pairs with nine demand-censored pairs; NoFuel was 0%, furnace-attributed clearing crossings were 0, and forest mass residual was 0.000 L. The every-mature-window gate therefore remains unmet; the early-ramp vs sustained-operation interpretation is still a decision point, and the 15% target has not been changed.
- The corrected gate was screened on four matched seeds (`20261054`, `20261024`, `20261023`, `20261027`) for 64 seasons at 200 people; output is in [m12-wp8c-fief-fuel-power-fix-output-2026-11-14.txt](m12-wp8c-fief-fuel-power-fix-output-2026-11-14.txt). The earliest eligible rolling 12-season shares were 16.8%, 13.9%, 15.9% and 3.6%; two of four exceed 15%, so the early-window gate still misses on this targeted cohort. All four had late windows wholly inside the final half (ending season 44 or later); their maximum was 1.6%, with zero late-window misses. All four had a mature work-share window; three met the charcoal-demand minimum and passed with zero fuel shortfall, while one was censored for low demand. NoFuel remained 0%, there were zero furnace-attributed clearings and the wood-ledger residual was 0.000 L. This points to a production-ramp miss under the current “every window after first kit + 12” rule, while the sampled late windows are well below the ceiling. It is a targeted diagnostic, not a broad pass; keep the 15% target and furnace multipliers unchanged pending a decision about whether the ceiling includes the first eligible ramp window.
