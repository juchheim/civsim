# WP-E5-12 uncoached Fief session kit

**Use only after** WP-E5-11 has promoted all 50 E5 rows and the integrated Fief view is available. This kit prepares the sessions; it does not count as participant evidence or close the 5-of-8 gate.

**Facilitator-only protocol. Do not show or send this document, scoring rubric, or examples to participants.** The participant-facing script is limited to the quoted task, the two quoted questions, and the brief non-coaching response below. Keep the rubric/examples out of sight until the participant has finished both answers and the verbatim record is saved.

## Session setup

- Recruit eight participants who have not been coached on the E5 mechanics.
- Use the same final build and pack for every participant. Record both hashes before the first session and verify they do not change between sessions.
- Assign an anonymous participant ID. Do not collect names.
- Record prior strategy-game familiarity using the same brief prompt for everyone: “How familiar are you with strategy games? Please describe your experience in a sentence or two.”
- Use the same natural-start task and a 25-minute wall-clock play period for everyone. The timebox is a facilitation choice aligned with the era's 25-minute aim; it does not change the pass threshold or scoring criteria.
- Do not explain mill allocation, fuel, faith, money, or other E5 mechanics during play. If asked, say: “Please make the choice you would make on your own; I can’t explain game systems during this session.”
- Do not point participants toward particular buildings, resources, research, or screens. Record technical interruptions and elapsed time; do not coach after an interruption.
- Keep the answer-bearing scoring rules and examples below private. Do not paraphrase them, preview likely answers, or give feedback about whether an answer is correct.

## Exact participant task

Read this prompt verbatim:

> Start a new game using the natural start. Play as you normally would for 25 minutes. Explore and make whatever choices you wish. I won’t explain game systems during the session. When the time is up, stop and answer two questions about what you observed.

Start the timer when the participant gains control. At 25 minutes, ask them to stop. If the participant stops early or a technical issue interrupts play, record the elapsed time and reason; keep the participant in the eight-person denominator and score only what they demonstrated without hints.

If the participant asks for help with game systems, repeat only: “Please make the choice you would make on your own; I can’t explain game systems during this session.” Do not answer questions about controls with strategic advice; resolve only technical issues needed to continue and record any interruption.

## Exact post-play questions

Ask both questions verbatim and in this order. Do not follow up with leading questions or clarify mechanics.

1. “What limited production or work in one town you observed, and what made it a bottleneck?”
2. “Describe one effect you observed from a faith or money system.”

Record each response verbatim. If the participant asks what a term means, repeat the question once without explanation.

## Scoring rules

Apply this rubric privately only after the participant has given both responses and their words are recorded verbatim. Do not display this section during the session. Score each participant **Pass** only if, without hints, their responses demonstrate both of the following:

- **Mill/fuel constraint:** one real constraint, such as a mill requiring an eligible site and power, shared power-hours limiting competing jobs, or a blast furnace needing charcoal.
- **Faith or money effect:** one real effect present in the tested build, such as staffed faith/learning institutions affecting knowledge or a trade claim collected from non-food goods after the protected food reserve.

If the participant did not encounter an effect, score that category **Not demonstrated**. Do not teach the mechanic and then count a prompted answer. A technically interrupted or early-ended session remains in the denominator; record context and score only demonstrated evidence.

The gate passes only if **at least 5 of 8** participants pass both criteria. Preserve all eight response records and scoring rationales, including failures. Do not change the threshold after sessions begin.

## Recruitment invitation template

Use this invitation to recruit volunteers. Arrange scheduling separately from the anonymous response records; do not copy participant names or contact details into the cohort data.

> I’m looking for eight volunteers for a short playtest of a new strategy game build. You’ll start a new game, play on your own for 25 minutes, and then answer two open questions about what you noticed. I won’t explain game systems during the session. No prior strategy-game experience is required. Your answers will be recorded under an anonymous participant ID without your name. You may stop at any time. If you’re interested, reply to arrange a time.

No participants have been recruited or tested as part of this preparation. Complete the records only from real sessions; never synthesize responses or results.
