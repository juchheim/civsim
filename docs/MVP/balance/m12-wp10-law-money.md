# M12 WP-E5-7 (first slice): law and money

**Status:** The user-approved WP-E5-7 rules are implemented as draft behavior and covered by focused regression tests. Numeric values remain hypotheses; this is not balance signoff or WP-E5-11 promotion. Fief-only state is appended to Fief snapshot/hash serialization; Bronze and Iron retain their existing schema and behavior.

## Implemented slice

- GOV-11 adds one administrative span through its existing typed effect. After GOV-11, a settlement reaching City tier automatically receives the catalogued 1.1 city-growth multiplier. The charter is derived from completed GOV-11 plus City tier; no separate saved charter flag is needed. This growth effect is Fief-only.
- GOV-10 performs at most one keyed check per live settlement-year, using existing cohesion disorder inputs. The current fit hypothesis is a 1% base chance plus 0.01 percentage point per disorder, capped at 10%; completed GOV-10 halves the event chance. An event reduces cohesion by one existing cohesion point (0.006). These numeric values are draft fit hypotheses pending cohort and balance evidence, not approved/accepted values. The keyed stream does not consume shared random state.
- TRD-08 records an origination-valued claim for the unpaid seller-requested barter value. Locked origination unit values convert later non-food goods into partial payments. Each season, claims collect goods already held after protecting the forecast food need plus one season reserve; no interest accrues. Claims survive route termination, can be collected locally, are written off when the debtor settlement is lost, and expire after eight seasons without payment.
- The claim ledger is separate from shared Compact state and is appended only in Fief trade snapshot/hash sections. Focused tests cover arrival with full cargo delivery and an unpaid claim, food protection, partial existing-goods transfer, snapshot continuation, local collection after compact termination, debtor-settlement loss write-off, and eight-season expiry.
- GOV-10 coverage selects a seeded roll between the untreated and halved chances; it proves an actual cohesion incident in the control match and its suppression by GOV-10 on the matched key. The test also verifies the legacy Iron gate. The earlier test skipped all settlements because its fixture had no CivicState; it now uses the fixture helper that initializes one.
- TRD-09 `admin_waste ×0.9` now reduces by 10% the work cost of live Administrator/Governor staffing in Fief. The quantity is each role's actual season work (`WorkCapacity - care work - fire work`) already assigned to the `Administer` task and deducted from settlement work allocation; staff and their administrative service remain in place, and the released share returns to the ordinary work pool. No new persistent state is needed. `FiefDoubleEntryTests` verifies the saving for a civilization with TRD-09, no saving for another Fief civilization, and no effect in Iron or Bronze. This remains a draft mechanical fit, not balance acceptance.

## Remaining

- Bronze/Iron snapshot identity is covered by the mode-isolation checks: non-Fief snapshots keep their prior schema, while an empty Fief claim ledger adds only its two Fief-only counters.
- Check GOV-10 incident presentation in the player-facing event feed when that view surface is available; the current minimal rule changes cohesion through the existing economy API.
- **SOC-11 is implemented as a Fief-only, observable draft model** ([detail and focused coverage](m12-wp10-soc11-guild-craft.md)). Non-kit recipes record a 5% input-loss hypothesis, SOC-11 reduces that allowance by its 1.2 factor, and craft-role training exposure is tracked at ×1.5; output, labor rate, tool durability, and T12 service life remain unchanged. Fief snapshot/hash serialization carries the added readouts, with older Fief versions migrated. These quantities remain balance hypotheses, not acceptance or promotion.
- **TRD-09 `trade_profit ×1.2` now improves the physical Fief invoice for the seller.** At dispatch, the seller's current `UnitValue` per cargo unit is frozen as `SellerBookValue`; when the seller has TRD-09, the agreed per-unit invoice increases by 20% of its positive spread over that frozen basis. Zero or negative spreads and non-Fief trade keep the existing invoice. Payment uses the ordinary existing-goods rules, including the food reserve and accepted TRD-08 claim path; cargo delivery and returns remain bounded by actual settlement, and no goods or coin are minted. The seller ledger records the realized margin once as `(value of goods actually received as payment − delivered cargo × frozen seller basis)` and does not apply a second multiplier. The 1.2 factor remains a draft hypothesis, not a balance gate or promoted value. Focused tests cover seller-vs-buyer research scoping, invoice payment and cargo delivery, frozen-basis use after stock changes, snapshot round trips, and Iron isolation.
- **DIP-08 has a Fief-only draft implementation**; see [the DIP-08 inheritance and succession follow-up](m12-wp10-dip08-inheritance.md). Focused tests cover peaceful Free Folk inheritance, leader succession, alliance retention/lapse, snapshot persistence, and Iron/Bronze isolation. Leader election uses a neutral seniority rule (oldest eligible living adult, then lowest FigureId), not a historical family model; spouse/kinship links remain deferred. The 2% inheritance chance, two-season confirmation interval, and seniority convention remain draft, and broader inheritance frequency/consequence fitting is still required before promotion.
